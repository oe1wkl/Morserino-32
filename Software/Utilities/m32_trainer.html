How the trainer works

The M32 Trainer plays Morse on your Morserino, you copy it — typed here or keyed on the paddle — and the page checks your copy, keeps a record of every character and decides what comes next. You need a computer with a browser that can talk to a serial port (Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a desktop) and firmware 10 or newer (serial protocol 1.6). Connect the M32 with its USB cable and press Connect: the page starts the M32's CW Keyer and plays through it (if the Echo Trainer is running, it is left alone and logged until you press Enter).

  • What is played. Characters: your Koch lesson (as set on the M32, also with Custom Chars), the M32's Practice Set, or all characters. Practise: groups, real words, phrases (word chains, plain language, QSO lines, your own), and — with all characters — call signs and prefixes made exactly as the M32 makes them.
  • Answering. Type what you heard and press Enter, or choose Keyed on the paddle and key it back. A miss shows what it was, plays it again, and brings it back a few items later. Enter starts, checks and goes on; Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter on a Mac) replays (that counts as help); Esc pauses.
  • Boxes. Each character climbs five boxes as you copy it right; a miss sends it two boxes down, and characters in low boxes come up more often. When all of your lesson's characters reach box 3 the page offers the next lesson — it never moves on by itself. Keyed answers, call signs and the Echo log are counted on their own and move no box.
  • Speed. The characters always sound at the speed set on the M32 (turn its knob; the page follows). Effective speed stretches the gaps between characters and words (Farnsworth) for this session only.
  • Echo Trainer log. Run the M32's Echo Trainer while the page is connected and every word it checks is logged below, with your reaction and keying times as the M32 measured them.
  • What changes on your M32. Only the Koch sequence and lesson, and only when you change them here or accept the next lesson. Speed, spacing and the screen setting are for the session; leaving the CW Keyer or disconnecting ends them. The transmitter is never keyed.
  • Your history stays in this browser, nowhere else. Export it now and then; import it on another computer.

The user manual has a section on the M32 Trainer: all Morserino-32 user manuals.
